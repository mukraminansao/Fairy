All six collections contain 12 distinct products. Each collection uses one shoppable photograph, and each product opens its own close-up. The original fashion layout still follows the [fashion reference](../../assets/references/02_fairy_fashion_shoppable.png). The [makeup reference](../../assets/references/03_fairy_makeup_shoppable.png) still guides Soft Rose, with three added products above the original arrangement and a wider vertical crop to include them.

Wear this set loads a complete women's outfit. Add to look builds a mix one item at a time. Rose Occasion and Sage Layers show relaxed arms in the 2D Front and Back views. The street mannequin switches to the visible-hands pose when a watch, bracelet, or ring is selected. Head & neck and Hands & wrists show the accessories up close. Headphones are visible from either side, and glasses are shown from the front.

The six swatch colors were sampled from the Soft Rose photograph in pan order. The eyeshadow simulator applies the chosen shade to the model's eyelids while preserving the other areas of the look. Lighting and the sample model's skin affect how the overlay reads. The selected shade carries into the Beauty Studio and persists after reload.

Built-in ImageGen created the [set photos](../../public/assets/sets/), separate [fashion](../../public/assets/fashion/items/) and [beauty](../../public/assets/makeup/items/) close-ups, and the updated [wardrobe artwork](../../public/assets/fashion/wardrobe/). The fashion prompts requested exactly 12 separated pieces for each coordinated rose evening and sage modest outfit. The beauty prompts requested 12 separated cosmetics across four rows for Warm Bronze and Berry Muse. The Soft Rose edit preserved the nine existing items and added a rosewood liner, a rose velour puff, and a rose-gold brow gel above them. The individual photo prompts requested matching packaging on warm travertine in isolated, independently lit cells. The wardrobe prompts specified full-length, high-resolution front and back images, visible hands, and the track jacket's R and Better Days Ahead graphics.
