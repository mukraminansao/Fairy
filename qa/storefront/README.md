# FAIRY storefront review

[Catalog desktop](catalog-desktop.png) · [Product desktop](product-desktop.png) · [Catalog tablet](catalog-768.png) · [Catalog phone](catalog-390.png) · [Product phone](product-390.png)

The storefront uses the approved shared header, warm background, typography and product photography. There is no standalone catalog or product page reference in the handoff. The desktop grid shows four products per row, with three at tablet widths and two on phones. Product photography retains its square source framing. Variant controls and the product description stack below the photo on phones.

Home and menu category links lead to the catalog. Search, category, price, sorting and pagination can be shared by URL. Product pages show dedicated photos, prices and catalog details, and link to the selected item in its shoppable scene when available.

The pendant's finishes and the lipstick's shades use their own SKU and demo stock limit. Bag rows include product photos, unit prices, line totals, variant labels and quantity controls. Existing browser data is still readable, and the guest account links to saved products, the bag and the studios. The catalog prices and quantities are demo fixtures. Account sign-in, persistent inventory and checkout belong to the backend phase.

[Catalog action confirmation](catalog-confirmation.png) shows the aligned buttons and feedback beside the item while the header is offscreen. The added quantity updates on repeat clicks. On phones the message sits above the stacked buttons. Clear filters resets the visible price and sort controls as well as the results.
