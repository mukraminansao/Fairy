## Renewal Cream prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create a new standalone square FAIRY skincare product photograph for a premium ecommerce catalog. Image 1 is packaging reference; image 2 is lighting, framing and photographic style reference. Rebuild the specified package as an isolated product photo on warm pearl travertine with champagne light and soft shadow; do not crop, cut out, or reproduce the group photo. Keep the entire item comfortably inside the square, front label sharp and legible, luxury retail macro photography. One single package only. No other products, captions, watermark, measurements, decorative lettering, hands, or loose cap. Subject: Renewal Cream, the low wide squat rounded frosted pearl glass jar at right in reference image 1, with a smooth broad matte rose-champagne screw lid. Show a slightly elevated front angle with a clean view of the lid and a creamy ivory finish. Add a softly out-of-focus blush petal far off to the side if useful for the lighting style. Exact black label text on the container only: “FAIRY” in serif capitals, then “RENEWAL CREAM” in smaller letterspaced sans serif capitals.
