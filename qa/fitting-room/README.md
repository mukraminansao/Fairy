# Fashion fitting room review

[Desktop at 1672 × 941](desktop.png) · [768px tablet](768.png) · [390px phone](390.png) · [Pendant and hijab detail](hijab-pendant.png)

The standalone fitting room carries the typography and palette from the [approved shoppable fashion screen](../../assets/references/02_fairy_fashion_shoppable.png). The handoff has no standalone fitting room reference. The desktop view fits the full mannequin, and tablet and phone retain the Front, Back, Head & neck, and Hands & wrists controls.

Save outfit stores a numbered snapshot in the current browser. Several outfits can be saved independently of the working look, and saving the same combination twice reuses its existing entry. Loading restores the selected pieces and pendant finish. Add outfit to bag adds one of each selected piece and lists the additions. Black inlay and Silver finish can appear as separate lines in the demo bag. Prices are computed from the catalog.

The browser checks cover changes in garment slots, accessories and sling bag visibility, the pendant over the hijab, local saves and reloads, variant totals, keyboard interaction, and tablet and phone layouts. The audit also compared the latest 1672 × 941 desktop screenshots for the homepage, Monochrome Street, Soft Rose Edit, and Beauty Studio with their reviewed captures. All four matched.

[Photo-based Monochrome Street accessories](photo-accessories-street.png) and [Rose Occasion and Sage Layers close-ups](photo-accessories-womens.png) show the revised layers in the browser. Most layers use masked catalog photos. The silver pendant finish is a digital recolor of the original pendant photo.

The built-in ImageGen tool made [three transparent wearable angle assets](../../public/assets/fashion/wardrobe/accessories/) using the two original catalog close-ups as references. The prompt requested a 2 × 2 transparent contact sheet, with straight-on front and rear views of the same black headphones with padded arch and brushed silver hinges, a straight-on view of the same black wraparound shield sunglasses, and one empty cell. It specified transparent negative space, unobstructed edges, soft product reflections, and no head or mannequin.

[Placement review](placement-review.png) shows the adjusted earring hooks, comb, stole, shoes, wrist placement and bag grip. The fit was checked in the illustrated front and rear poses. The mannequin remains a fixed 2D pose, so cloth folds and grip are approximations.
