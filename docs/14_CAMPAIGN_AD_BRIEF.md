# FAIRY campaign ad brief

A 15-second fashion and beauty spot for the homepage, with a 16:9 master and room to crop for phones. The direction is warm champagne light, black fabric, restrained cuts, and tactile sound. Each shot features a visible action with the same model and the same products throughout.

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0–4 sec | A hand twists up the Soft Mocha lipstick, then applies a single stroke. Follow the motion in a tight close-up. |
| 4–8 sec | Match cut to the hand pulling up the black jacket zipper. The silver pendant swings as the model turns into the light. |
| 8–12 sec | Cut to the fragrance sprayer depressing. Backlight the mist so it moves across the frame while the bottle stays in focus. |
| 12–15 sec | Hold on the model for a beat, then add the FAIRY wordmark and “Everything you love. Beautifully together.” in the edit. |

Record or generate moving footage for each shot. Use the approved product photos as continuity references, including the lipstick shade, jacket markings, pendant shape, and fragrance bottle. Add the brand text in post to keep spelling and typography exact. A quiet instrumental bed can sit under the zipper, lipstick click, and perfume spray. Leave room for muted web playback.

Review the rendered ad for hand and product continuity before adding the player below “Shop by world.”
